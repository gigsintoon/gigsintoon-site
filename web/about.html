About Gigs in toon

Gigs in toon is run by Micky, a music fan from Gateshead who has spent decades buying albums and CD singles and going to gigs in Newcastle and beyond.

Every day @gigsintoon marks the anniversaries of the records that matter to me – mostly punk, ska, emo, rock and 90s British guitar music – plus the new releases I'm excited about. This site collects all of those posts in one place, so you can pick any day of the year and see what came out on it, or search for a band and see everything of theirs I've written about.

What's here

  • On this day – every release for a date, newest first, with the covers from my own collection.
  • Favourites – my albums of the year, and what's coming up.
  • Gig memories – tickets and setlists from gigs I've been to (coming soon).
