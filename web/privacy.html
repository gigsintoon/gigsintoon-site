Privacy & cookies

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, doesn't build a profile of you and doesn't sell or share anything. You don't need an account to use it.

Cookies

Visitors get no cookies. The only cookie the site ever sets is a sign-in cookie for the site owner, used to manage the content. It's strictly necessary, so there's nothing to accept or decline.

Visitor numbers

To see whether anyone is using the site, each page view adds one to a daily count. All that's kept is the date, the page, the name of the website that linked you here (if any) and whether it was a phone or a computer. No IP address, cookie or other identifier is stored, so the counts can't be linked to you.

Hosting

The site runs on Microsoft Azure in the UK and Europe. Like any web server, Azure briefly processes your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the service; that isn't used by this site.

Images and links

Album and single covers are shown to illustrate the releases being written about and remain the property of their owners. If you own an image and want it removed, get in touch and it'll be taken down. Links to other sites (X, Instagram, Apple Music and so on) are covered by those sites' own policies.
